# 8. Constraints, validation, and growth

## Database-enforced rules in the current schema

- `id`: UUID primary key, generated by `gen_random_uuid()`.
- Required fields: `NOT NULL`.
- `quest_id`: `CHECK` allowlist of `playground-partition`, `classroom-meme`, and `midday-meal`.
- `participant_count`: between 5 and 40.
- `vote_count`: at least 5 and no more than participant count.
- `winning_vote_count`: at least 1 and no more than vote count.
- `majority_percentage`: integer between 1 and 100.
- `session_date`: required, defaults to `current_date`.
- RLS enabled; privileges revoked from `public`, `anon`, and `authenticated`; `service_role` gets select and insert.
- No `UNIQUE` constraint or foreign key is present.

The backend also checks the room completion state and five-vote/five-participant threshold before inserting. The `majority_percentage` consistency with the two vote counts is enforced by backend calculation only, not by a database constraint.

## Index and growth

The descending `session_date` index serves the recent summary query. There is no sharding or partitioning in the prototype. The API returns at most 50 rows and the client caches for 15 seconds. Likely first scaling pressure is the single-process in-memory live room state, which does not automatically coordinate across multiple Node servers. Measure load before choosing a shared state/realtime solution.
